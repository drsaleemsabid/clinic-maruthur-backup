Private backup integration

Clinic backups.
Under the clinic’s control.

Clinic Backup helps an authorised clinic administrator store encrypted recovery copies of clinic records and attachments in a Google Drive account they control.

Read the privacy policy →

What this integration does

  • Encrypts backup contents locally before uploading them.
  • Creates and manages its backup files in a private Google Drive folder.
  • Checks storage availability and verifies uploaded backup integrity.

Google authorisation is requested by the installed backup application, not by this website. The integration requests limited per-file Drive access, not unrestricted access to the account’s entire Drive.
